# Recipe App

A mobile app for saving recipes from short-form videos, planning what to cook, and keeping track of ingredients, alone or with friends.

## Recipes

**Recipe**:
A dish one user has saved, with its ingredients and steps, usually drawn from a Source Video. Each Recipe belongs to exactly one user; others save their own copy.
_Avoid_: Post, save, bookmark

**Tag**:
A label on a Recipe (cuisine, meal type, "quick") used to find it later.
_Avoid_: Folder, category, collection

**Want to Cook**:
The Recipes a user has marked as ones they intend to make soon.
_Avoid_: Queue, wishlist, favorites

**Source Video**:
The TikTok, Reel, Short or other short-form video a Recipe was saved from.
_Avoid_: Link, post, clip

**Draft**:
A Recipe the app has filled in from a Source Video's caption that the user has not yet reviewed.
_Avoid_: Import, suggestion

## Ingredients

**Ingredient**:
A plain, unquantified food item such as "garlic", which Recipe lines, the Pantry and Shopping Lists all refer to.
_Avoid_: Item, product, food

**Pantry**:
The Ingredients a user currently has on hand, recorded as present or absent, never by amount.
_Avoid_: Inventory, fridge, have-list

**Staple**:
An Ingredient a user always treats as being in their Pantry, such as salt or oil.
_Avoid_: Basics, essentials

**Shopping List**:
The things a user needs to buy: Ingredients from Recipes plus anything added by hand. A user can share their Shopping List with chosen Friends, but it stays theirs.
_Avoid_: Grocery list, need-list, cart

## Cooking together

**Friend**:
Another user with whom a user has a mutual, accepted connection. Friends see each other's Rankings and Cook Logs.
_Avoid_: Follower, contact, connection

**Cook Plan**:
A shared space where Friends choose Recipes to make together and gather the Ingredients for them on the Cook Plan's own list.
_Avoid_: Session, event, group

**Claim**:
A Cook Plan member's promise to buy a particular ingredient on that Cook Plan's list.
_Avoid_: Assignment, task

## Rating

**Cook Log**:
A record of one time a user cooked a Recipe: when, with whom, notes and photos.
_Avoid_: Review, entry, history

**Ranking**:
A user's personal ordered list of the Recipes they have cooked, built by comparing each new one against those already ranked.
_Avoid_: Rating, leaderboard

**Score**:
The 0–10 number a Recipe gets from its place in a user's Ranking.
_Avoid_: Stars, rating
