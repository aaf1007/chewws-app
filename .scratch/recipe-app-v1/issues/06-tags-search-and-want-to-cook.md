# 06: Tags, search and Want to Cook

**What to build:** Drafts come with suggested Tags (cuisine, meal type, "quick") from the Draft builder. Users can add, remove and create their own Tags, which are per user. They can search their Recipes by title, Ingredient or Tag. They can mark Recipes as Want to Cook and see that list on its own.
